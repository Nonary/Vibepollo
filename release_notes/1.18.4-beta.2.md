# Vibepollo 1.18.4-beta.2 - 2026-08-07

Notice: Vibepollo may trigger false-positive antivirus alerts. Details and workarounds are documented here: https://github.com/Nonary/Vibepollo/issues/59

## Fixes
- Prevented virtual-display startup and session transitions from racing physical-display restoration, blocking cold startup, retaining probe displays, or applying a virtual HDR profile to a physical fallback.
- Reactivated compatible inactive virtual displays during recovery and recreated missing refresh-mode descriptors instead of unnecessarily replacing the monitor.
