# Vibepollo 1.18.3-stable.5 - 2026-08-06

Notice: Vibepollo may trigger false-positive antivirus alerts. Details and workarounds are documented here: https://github.com/Nonary/Vibepollo/issues/59

## Fixes
- Prevented completed virtual-display sessions from reapplying their old exclusive layout after physical displays were restored. Recovery workers now disarm only after every stream owner exits, while the virtual display remains available for a clean restore and future sessions.
